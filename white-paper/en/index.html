White Paper 1.0: the concept of the International Museum of Water Towers

The International Museum of Water Towers: research concept, digital encyclopedia of the world’s engineering heritage and development programme. Public cultural edition, November 2026.

Read the White Paper (PDF, 10 pp., in Ukrainian)

Summary

The International Museum of Water Towers (IMWT) is an initiative to create an international museum and an open digital research infrastructure dedicated to the world’s water towers. Its physical starting point is the historic water tower in Mala Vilshanka, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine. The digital museum will gradually bring together information on towers of different eras, continents, types and states of preservation.

IMWT’s mission is to discover, document, research, explain and help preserve water towers as evidence of the history of engineering thought, urban life, industry, railways, architecture and collective memory. Its principle: every tower has its own biography; together, the towers form a still unexplored map of the development of technical civilisation.

What is new about IMWT is the attempt to join scattered local, national, technical and museum knowledge into one multilingual, verifiable and open global system: a digital passport for every tower, explicit levels of evidence (documented fact, interpretation, hypothesis) and open data.
