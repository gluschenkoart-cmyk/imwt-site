Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 November 2026. Applies to the 4chess app for Android and the 4chess website.

In short: 4chess has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never sells data. We keep only what is needed to sign you in, store your rated games and show the ladder. You can delete your account and all its data at any time.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is LLC “International Museum of Water Towers” (LLC IMWT), EDRPOU 46078324, Kyiv, Ukraine (“we”). Contact for any privacy question or request: gluschenko.art@gmail.com.

2. Playing without an account

Games against the engine and games on one device work without an account and without internet. Those games, your settings and unsent results stay only on your device.

3. What we collect when you have an account

  • Sign-in data from the method you choose (Google Play Games, Google, Facebook, Apple, Telegram, or a guest account): the provider's account identifier, and — if the provider shares them — e-mail address, name and profile picture URL. For a guest account we store only a secure hash of a random key kept on your device.
  • Profile: your display name (public on the ladder), language, profile picture URL.
  • Rated games: the rules, every move of the game, places, scores, rating changes, the version of the engine, and when the game started and finished.
  • Ratings: your rating, number of games, best rating and ladder position in each game type.
  • Sessions and security: hashed sign-in tokens, device type and name, creation and expiry times; the IP address is used for rate limiting and appears in server logs.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, device advertising ID or payment data. 4chess has no in-app purchases.

4. Why we use it

  • to create and keep your account and sign you in automatically (performance of our agreement with you);
  • to referee rated games, calculate ratings and show the public ladder (performance of the agreement);
  • to protect the service and fair play: detecting tampered games and abuse (our legitimate interest);
  • to answer your requests (performance of the agreement / legitimate interest).

5. What is public

On the ladder other players can see your display name, profile picture, rating, number of games and position. Nothing else about you is shown to other players.

6. Who processes data for us

  • our cloud hosting provider (servers and database in the European Union);
  • the sign-in provider you choose (Google, Meta/Facebook, Apple, Telegram) — only to confirm your identity, under their own privacy policies;
  • GitHub (hosting of this website).

We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

7. How long we keep it

Account, games and ratings are kept until you delete the account. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days. Expired sign-in sessions are removed automatically.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: Account → Delete account — deletion is immediate and removes your account, linked sign-in methods, sessions, games and ratings. Without the app: request deletion here; we complete it within 30 days.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction, export or deletion of your data, and object to processing, by writing to gluschenko.art@gmail.com. You may also complain to a data protection authority — in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights; in the EU, the authority of your country.

10. Children

4chess is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it.

11. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes; access tokens expire after 15 minutes.

12. Changes

We will publish changes on this page and update the date above; important changes will also be announced in the app.
